How it’s done

What a deep cleaning looks like

  1. Cover and protect

    The indoor unit gets a cleaning bag and the wall below is covered in plastic, so the dirty water ends up in the bag, not on your wall or floor.

  2. Wash the coil and blower

    The coil and blower are washed out. That’s where dust and grime build up.

  3. The outdoor unit

    The outdoor coil can be cleaned too, so the system can get rid of heat properly. Ask AJ whether your system needs it.

Why it matters

A clean system cools better

A dirty coil and a clogged blower make your system work harder and cool less. Regular maintenance also catches small problems before they turn into repairs.

Ask AJ how often your system needs it. It depends on the unit and how hard it runs.
