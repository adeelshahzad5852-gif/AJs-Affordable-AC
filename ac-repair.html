When to call

Signs your AC needs a look

  • It runs but doesn’t cool, or blows warm air
  • It won’t turn on at all
  • Water is dripping from the indoor unit
  • There’s ice on the pipes or the coil
  • It makes a new noise, or smells bad when it starts
  • A light is blinking or an error code is showing

Honest advice

Repair or replace? You’ll get a straight answer.

Sometimes a repair is the right call. Sometimes an old unit isn’t worth fixing. AJ finds the fault, explains what each option would cost and lets you decide.

Before you call

Have these ready

  1. Where you are

    Your town or neighborhood, so the visit can be planned.

  2. What it’s doing

    Not cooling, not turning on, leaking, noisy or showing an error code.

  3. The brand and age

    The brand of the unit and roughly how old it is, if you know.
